A quick prayer...

I surrender all of who I think I am today and all of what I think I am here to do, and open myself up to allow the Universe to express itself through me. Show me where to go, what to do, what words to use when I get there and when to be silent.

I surrender all of the identities and ideas that stand in the way of my compassion, anything that would prohibit me from promoting the expression of Life in all of its creativity and wonder. Help me to see any pain I experience as sensation, not a storyline.

I wish to Love fully and completely today in the absence of fear, and honor my opportunity by living each moment in the supreme present, whether my heart may be breaking or bursting.

Jaymee Carpenter
